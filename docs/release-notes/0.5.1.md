# Sagrada 0.5.1

- Click a workspace indicator to focus it. Click the focused workspace again to toggle its persistent Tiling / Floating policy.
- Right-click any indicator, or Control-click, to open the full Sagrada dropdown, including workspace layout settings, config, screenshots, keep-awake, scroll reversal and Quit.
- Five persistent workspaces (1–5) are now the default for version-2 configs without an explicit `persistent-workspaces` list. Explicit lists remain configurable.
- Indicator drawing and click hit areas share the same point-based geometry. AppKit template images adapt to menu-bar appearance and Retina scale.
- Visible glyph ink is centered horizontally and vertically inside the outline, rather than centering the asymmetric font metrics. Tests cover digits 1–9 at both occupancy weights.

Interactive status items require macOS 14.4+. Earlier systems retain the existing SwiftUI menu-only fallback. Ad-hoc signed local builds may require renewed Accessibility approval after installation.
